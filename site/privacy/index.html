Sphere - Course Review App (BETA) privacy policy

THIS EXTENSION IS FOR BETA TESTING

Effective date / policy version: 7 November 2026 / 2026-11-07. Contact rcadavies@gmail.com; see support.

Data and purpose

Sphere handles reviewer name, email, role, and approval status; authentication, session, reconnect, and device credentials; course/page URLs and activity/SCORM identifiers, titles, and current location; comments, replies, statuses, timestamps, selected text, visual anchors, and optional screenshots and attachments submitted only at the reviewer's request; and browser/build, navigation, reliability, and error diagnostics. It uses these only to authenticate authorised reviewers and let course teams record, discuss, navigate to and resolve contextual feedback, support and secure the beta, and improve that single purpose.

When you save a comment in a Moodle quiz preview, Sphere can attach a screenshot automatically if the capture option shown in the composer is checked and you have opened Sphere’s Chrome menu to grant temporary access to that tab. You can uncheck the option before saving. Sphere does not capture learner attempts or take screenshots in the background. The image may show visible quiz questions, answers or feedback and is shared under the comment’s access rules.

Sharing, access and security

Data travels over HTTPS to the configured review service and sites being reviewed. Authorised course-team members may read course-review content. Service operator staff do not routinely read it and may access specific data only on explicit user request about that data, for security or abuse, or legal obligation. Internal operational or improvement use is limited to aggregated and anonymised data. Data is not sold or used for advertising, creditworthiness or lending. Sphere executes no remote code.

Course-access and review notifications are sent through the operator’s Gmail account. Depending on the notification and the recipient’s approved role, emails can include reviewer name and email, course/page location, comment text and a course link. Google processes these messages for delivery. The isolated fictional Store-review demo does not send email.

GitHub Pages receives ordinary request metadata: IP address, user agent, request time, and requested path. Sphere sends it no Moodle content, review content, credentials, or tokens; latest.json is fixed version metadata only.

Retention, deletion and Limited Use

Review records remain until deleted through authorised service controls. Sign-out removes local session/device credentials. Decline keeps the reviewer signed out and prevents course access. Sphere complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including Limited Use: use, transfer and human access are limited to its disclosed course-review purpose and permitted support, security and legal exceptions. A changed policy requires fresh acknowledgement.
