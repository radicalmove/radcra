# Getting started with Sphere

Sphere is for approved reviewers. You need access to the Moodle course as well as approval to review it in Sphere. Installing the extension does not give you either.

## Install and open your course

1. In the Chrome profile you use for Moodle, open the [Sphere Chrome Web Store listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/sphere-course-review-app/mohajifpffgbmkelnpdibcebmjpjngak) and choose **Add to Chrome**. If the Store copy is already installed, keep it.
2. Open the course link supplied by your course team and sign in to Moodle. Refresh the course if it was already open when you installed Sphere.
3. Read the first-run notice and choose **Continue** if you agree. Look for the Sphere panel at the bottom right. If it’s missing, check that Sphere is installed, enabled and not paused.

## Request access for the first time

For a course using Sphere’s email approval flow:

1. In the Sphere panel, choose **Sign in → Request access**.
2. Enter your name, email address and requested role. Submit the request. No Sphere password is needed for this flow.
3. Wait for your administrator to approve the request and assign your course role.
4. Open the approval/confirmation email in the **same Chrome profile** where you requested access and choose **Confirm email** within 20 minutes. Access emails can take a few minutes because they are sent by a scheduled worker. Check spam as well as your inbox.
5. Return to Moodle. In Sphere, choose **Sign in → Check access**. When you see **Connected**, you can start reviewing.

Not every course uses this flow. If Sphere says the course isn’t enabled or that the email flow isn’t available for your address, contact your course team for the approved access route. Don’t create another account.

If you were approved but the email link expired, use **Sign in** with your approved email address to request a fresh link.

## Already approved, or moving from an older version

- **Updating a Store install:** Stay in your usual Chrome profile. Chrome updates the existing listing automatically. If Chrome disables Sphere after an update and you’re comfortable with the new site permission, choose **Accept permissions** to re-enable it. Refresh your Moodle course.
- **Moving from an unpacked pilot:** Install from the Store link, then disable the unpacked copy at `chrome://extensions` so only one Sphere runs. Your account, approved course roles and comments live on the server, but your browser sign-in may not carry over. Keep the old copy disabled rather than removing it until the Store copy works.
- **If you’re signed out:** Use the email address you were approved with. Where email sign-in is enabled, sign in with that address; a new Chrome profile may need an email confirmation link. If the course doesn’t offer that flow, ask the course team to reconnect your existing account. Don’t choose **Request access** just because you updated.

## Add a course for review

Ask the course team to send the course link and intended reviewer role to the Sphere administrator privately. An authorized administrator or learning designer registers the course in Sphere; reviewer roles are then approved separately. On Moodle or LEARN, each reviewer still needs access to the underlying course. Registration does not switch on email onboarding for every course.
